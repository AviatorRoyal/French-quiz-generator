# Quizzes don't have to be about French verbs. A question can carry its
# own hint text inline with "hint:" instead of pointing at a verb table.

id: sample-other-subject
subject: Sample
title: Any subject works
description: Proof the format isn't tied to conjugation — hints can be inline text.
tags: demo
---
type: mcq
prompt: What is the derivative of sin(x)?
choices: cos(x) | -cos(x) | sin(x) | -sin(x)
answer: cos(x)
hint: d/dx sin(x) = cos(x); d/dx cos(x) = -sin(x). The sign flips on the cosine.
why: Standard trig derivative.
